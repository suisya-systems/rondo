The line before a lap states its ceiling instead of predicting minutes (#22)

`performStep` announced the lap as "Sending one lap; this is the step that
takes minutes." Minutes is a unit rondo has no basis for: measured laps on
this machine answered in 18-21 s and the first dogfood lap died in 3.8 s, so
the sentence made a healthy lap look hung and a hung lap look healthy.

The line now says "Sending one lap; rondo will wait <n> ms for it, the ceiling
the plan declared", where <n> is the plan's `invocationCeilingMs` -- the
operator's declared patience, which `performStep` already holds and which is a
bound rondo is actually holding the lap to rather than a forecast of how long
the lap will take.

`test/refrain/interpreter.test.ts` gains a case that reads the report of a lap
reaching `performLap` and asserts it names the ceiling and does not say "takes
minutes", so the line cannot quietly go back to predicting a duration.

The transcript in `docs/operations/rondo-cli.md` still quotes the old line, and
so do the `docs/operations/lap-*-dogfood.md` records: those are transcripts of
runs that happened, and they are left as they stood on the day.

`npm run verify` observed EXIT=1. The failing step is `knip`, on package.json
findings that predate this change (unused devDependency `@biomejs/biome`,
unlisted binaries `tsc`, `biome`, `knip`, `vitest`) and that this two-file diff
does not touch. `build`, `lint` and `typecheck` each exited 0, and the full
suite is 815 passed, 1 skipped.
